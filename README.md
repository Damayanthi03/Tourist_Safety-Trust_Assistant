# 🛡️ Tourist Safety & Trust Assistant

### Smart India Hackathon 2026 | SIH26204

An AI-powered smart tourism platform that acts as a trusted digital travel companion, helping tourists stay safe, find verified services, manage their budget, avoid scams, and receive intelligent real-time assistance.

---

## 📌 Problem Statement

Tourists visiting unfamiliar destinations often face difficulties in finding trusted local services, verified information, and timely assistance during emergencies.

Common problems include:

- Unsafe travel routes
- Fake or misleading reviews
- Fraudulent hotel and booking information
- Unexpected travel expenses
- Lack of reliable real-time assistance

Our goal is to provide tourists with reliable, verified, and context-aware information that they can trust while travelling.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

The **Tourist Safety & Trust Assistant** combines safety, trust verification, budget management, and AI-powered assistance into one platform.

## 🔄 How It Works

The platform follows a simple end-to-end process:

1. **Register & Enter Destination**
   - Tourist creates an account/logs in.
   - Enters destination, travel dates, and preferences.

2. **AI Analyzes Requirements**
   - Analyzes travel requirements.
   - Checks safety risks and available real-time updates.

3. **Hotel & Booking Verification**
   - Reviews trusted hotels and services.
   - Checks ratings, reviews, and authenticity.
   - Verifies submitted hotel/business information.

4. **Budget Calculation**
   - Estimates total travel expenses.
   - Provides category-wise breakdown such as food, travel, and stay.

5. **AI Assistant & Emergency Support**
   - Provides real-time travel guidance.
   - Sends alerts during safety or emergency situations.

6. **Safe & Smart Travel Experience**
   - Provides a trusted, verified, personalized, and budget-friendly travel experience.

---

## 🏨 Hotel Trust Verification

Hotels or local businesses can submit their details for verification.

The system collects:

- Hotel/Business Name
- Category
- Tariff per Night
- Government Trade/Municipal License ID
- Physical Address
- Verified Safety Features
- Amenities

The submitted information can then go through an **Admin Trust Review** before the business is marked as trusted.

> Note: Demo license IDs used during development are sample data. In a real deployment, verification should use authorized/official sources.
